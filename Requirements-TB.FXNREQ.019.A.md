**Business process** (A) Registration: Activity: Check in the client: As a [Health worker or data entry clerk or medical office receptionist](ActorDefinition-healthworkerordataentryclerkormedicalofficereceptionist.md), I want to: 
>  
Provide a list of scheduled contacts to allow clients that are on TB preventive treatment or TB treatment who are lost to follow-up to be traced 
 
so that 
>  
I can track and follow up clients requiring TB care services
